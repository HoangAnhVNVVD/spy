Privacy & Redaction Protection

🛡️ Secure Local Filter

Your client-side privacy engine filters data before transmitting to this server:

  • Ignored Apps: Password managers (1Password, KeePass, Bitwarden) and lock screens are completely dropped or masked.
  • Private Browsing: InPrivate & Incognito window titles are scrubbed automatically.
  • Sensitive Redaction: Credit cards, emails, auth tokens, and passwords in window titles are replaced with [REDACTED].
  • Offline SQLite: Records buffer locally on your Windows PC and sync automatically when Railway connection is available.
